We, Haitham bin Tarik, the Sultan of Oman

after perusal of [the Basic Statute of the State](https://decree.om/2021/rd20210006/),

and [Royal Decree 57/2022 Reorganising the Oman Human Rights Commission](https://decree.om/2022/rd20220057/),

and [Royal Decree 76/2023 Appointing Members to the Oman Human Rights Commission](https://decree.om/2023/rd20230076/),

and in pursuance of public interest,

### have decreed as follows

### Article I

[The Statute of the Oman Human Rights Commission attached to the mentioned Royal Decree 57/2022](https://decree.om/2022/rd20220057/) is hereby replaced with the attached statute.

### Article II

The membership of the members of the Oman Human Rights Commission pursuant to the mentioned [Royal Decree 76/2023](https://decree.om/2023/rd20230076/) hereby continues until it is recomposed in accordance with the attached statute.

### Article III

All that is contrary to the attached statute, or in conflict with its provisions, is hereby repealed.

### Article IV

This decree must be published in the Official Gazette, and comes into force on the day following the date of its publication.

**Issued on: 26 Shawwal 1447Corresponding to: 14 April 2026**

**Haitham bin TarikSultan of Oman**

Published in [Official Gazette 1644 issued on 19 April 2026](https://qanoon.om/p/2026/og1644/).
