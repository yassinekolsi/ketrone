We, Haitham bin Tarik, the Sultan of Oman

after perusal of [the Basic Statute of the State](https://decree.om/2021/rd20210006/),

the [Nature Reserves and Wildlife Conservation Law issued by Royal Decree 6/2003](https://decree.om/2003/rd20030006/),

and [Royal Decree 6/2006 Establishing the Oman Botanic Garden](https://decree.om/2023/rd20230025/),

and in pursuance of public interest,

### have decreed as follows

### Article I

The texts of articles I and III of the mentioned [Royal Decree 6/2006](https://decree.om/2006/rd20060006/) are hereby replaced with the following texts:

### *Article I*

*The area specified in the attached map is hereby made a protected natural site named “Oman Botanic Garden”, subordinate to the Governorate of Muscat (Muscat Municipality).*

### *Article III*

*The Chairman of Muscat Municipality shall exercise all the powers stipulated in the mentioned Nature Reserves and Wildlife Conservation Law in regard to the Oman Botanic Garden, and he shall issue the regulations and decisions implementing this decree in coordination with the relevant entities.*

### Article II

This decree must be published in the Official Gazette, and comes into force on the date of its issuance.

**Issued on: 12 Shawwal 1447Corresponding to: 31 March 2026**

**Haitham bin TarikSultan of Oman**

Published in [Official Gazette 1642 issued on 5 April 2026](https://qanoon.om/p/2026/og1642/).
