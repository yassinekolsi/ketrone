We, Haitham bin Tarik, the Sultan of Oman

after perusal of [the Basic Statute of the State](https://decree.om/2021/rd20210006/),

and [the Law of the Diplomatic and Consular Corps issued by Royal Decree 22/2025](https://decree.om/2025/rd20250022/),

and in pursuance of public interest,

### have decreed as follows

### Article I

Ambassador Abdulghaffar bin Abdulkarim bin Issa Al\-Balushi, Our ambassador to the Federative Republic of Brazil, is hereby appointed a non\-resident ambassador extraordinary and plenipotentiary of Ours to the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela, the Republic of Paraguay, the Republic of Ecuador, and the Republic of Chile.

### Article II

This decree must be published in the Official Gazette, and comes into force on the date of its issuance.

**Issued on: 3 Dhu Al\-Qadah 1447Corresponding to: 21 April 2026**

**Haitham bin TarikSultan of Oman**

Published in [Official Gazette 1645 issued on 26 April 2026](https://qanoon.om/p/2026/og1645/).
