We, Haitham bin Tarik, the Sultan of Oman

after perusal of [the Basic Statute of the State](https://decree.om/2021/rd20210006/),

and the Air Services Agreement Between the Government of the Sultanate of Oman and the Government of the Republic of Zambia signed in the city of Punta Cana on 11 November 2025,

and in pursuance of public interest,

### have decreed as follows

### Article I

The mentioned agreement is hereby ratified in the form attached.

### Article II

This decree must be published in the Official Gazette, and comes into force on the date of its issuance.

**Issued on: 18 Ramadan 1447Corresponding to: 8 March 2026**

**Haitham bin TarikSultan of Oman**

Published in [Official Gazette 1639 issued on 15 March 2026](https://qanoon.om/p/2026/og1639/).
