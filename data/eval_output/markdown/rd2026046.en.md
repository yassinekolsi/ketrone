We, Haitham bin Tarik, the Sultan of Oman

after perusal of [the Basic Statute of the State](https://decree.om/2021/rd20210006/),

and [the Omani Nationality Law issued by Royal Decree 17/2025](https://decree.om/2025/rd20250017/),

and in pursuance of public interest,

### have decreed as follows

### Article I

Omani nationality is hereby granted to those named in the attached list.

### Article II

This decree must be published in the Official Gazette, and comes into force on the date of its issuance.

**Issued on: 24 Shawwal 1447Corresponding to: 12 April 2026**

**Haitham bin TarikSultan of Oman**

Published in [Official Gazette 1644 issued on 19 April 2026](https://qanoon.om/p/2026/og1644/).
