We, Haitham bin Tarik, the Sultan of Oman

after perusal of [the Basic Statute of the State](https://decree.om/2021/rd20210006/),

[the Statute of the Oman Medical Specialty Board issued by Royal Decree 31/2006](https://decree.om/2006/rd20060031/),

[Royal Decree 33/2013 Regarding the Medical and Allied Health Professions in Government Medical Establishments (Civil and Military)](https://decree.om/2013/rd20130033/),

[Royal Decree 18/2018 Establishing the Oman College of Health Sciences and the Higher Institute of Health Specialties and Issuing Their Statutes](https://decree.om/2018/rd20180018/),

and [the Statute of the Administrative Apparatus of the State issued by Royal Decree 75/2020](https://decree.om/2020/rd20200075/),

and in pursuance of public interest,

### have decreed as follows

### Article I

The Higher Institute of Health Specialties is hereby abolished.

### Article II

All mandates, allocations, assets, rights, obligations, records, and holdings of the Higher Institute of Health Specialties are hereby transferred to the Oman Medical Specialty Board.

### Article III

Those occupying teaching positions in the Higher Institute of Health Specialties are hereby transferred to the Oman Medical Specialty Board with their same employment status and financial grades, and the provisions of Schedule 5 attached to the mentioned [Royal Decree 33/2013](https://decree.om/2013/rd20130033/) hereby apply to them.

The officers of the Higher Institute of Health Specialties are also hereby transferred to the Oman Medical Specialty Board with their same financial grades.

### Article IV

The Board of Trustees of the Oman Medical Specialty Board shall undertake the mandates assigned to the Board of Trustees and the Academic Council of the Higher Institute of Health Specialties stipulated in the mentioned [Royal Decree 18/2018](https://decree.om/2018/rd20180018/).

### Article V

The academic programmes at the Higher Institute of Health Specialties hereby continue to operate for students enrolled in those programmes until their completion, and the certificates and academic degrees for these programmes must be approved by the Board of Trustees of the Oman Medical Specialty Board.

### Article VI

The Chairman of the Board of Trustees of the Oman Medical Specialty Board shall issue the necessary regulations and decisions for implementing the provisions of this decree, and until they are issued, the regulations and decisions in force in the Higher Institute of Health Specialties continue to operate.

### Article VII

All provisions relating to the Higher Institute of Health Specialties in the mentioned [Royal Decree 18/2018](https://decree.om/2018/rd20180018/) and the statute attached to it are hereby repealed, as well as all that is contrary to this decree or in conflict with its provisions.

### Article VIII

This decree must be published in the Official Gazette, and comes into force on the date of its issuance.

**Issued on: 4 Dhu Al\-Hijja 1447
Corresponding to: 21 May 2026**

**Haitham bin Tarik
Sultan of Oman**

Published in [Official Gazette 1649 issued on 26 May 2025](https://qanoon.om/p/2025/og1649/).
