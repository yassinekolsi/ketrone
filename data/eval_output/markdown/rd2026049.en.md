We, Haitham bin Tarik, the Sultan of Oman

after perusal of [the Basic Statute of the State](https://decree.om/2021/rd20210006/),

[the Civil Service Law issued by Royal Decree 120/2004](https://decree.om/2004/rd20040120/),

and [Royal Decree 7/2026 Establishing the Office of the Deputy Prime Minister for Economic Affairs, Determining Its Mandates, and Adopting Its Organisational Structure](https://decree.om/2026/rd20260007/),

and in pursuance of public interest,

### have decreed as follows

### Article I

Yarub bin Abdullah bin Saleh Al\-Yarubi is hereby appointed Secretary General of the Office of the Deputy Prime Minister for Economic Affairs with the special grade.

### Article II

This decree must be published in the Official Gazette, and comes into force on the date of its issuance.

**Issued on: 4 Dhu Al\-Qa’dah 1447
Corresponding to: 22 April 2026**

**Haitham bin Tarik
Sultan of Oman**

Published in [Official Gazette 1645 issued on 26 April 2026](https://qanoon.om/p/2026/og1645/).
