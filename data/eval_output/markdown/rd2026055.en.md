We, Haitham bin Tarik, the Sultan of Oman

after perusal of [the Basic Statute of the State](https://decree.om/2021/rd20210006/),

and the Agreement Between the Government of the Sultanate of Oman and the Government of the French Republic Concerning Employment Authorisation of Spouses of Employees of Official Missions signed in the city of Paris on 14 April 2026,

and in pursuance of public interest,

### have decreed as follows

### Article I

The mentioned agreement is hereby ratified in the form attached.

### Article II

This decree must be published in the Official Gazette, and comes into force on the date of its issuance.

**Issued on: 19 Dhu Al\-Qa'da 1447
Corresponding to: 7 May 2026**

**Haitham bin Tarik
Sultan of Oman**

Published in [Official Gazette 1647 issued on 10 May 2026](https://qanoon.om/p/2026/og1647/).
