We, Haitham bin Tarik, the Sultan of Oman

after perusal of [the Basic Statute of the State](https://decree.om/2021/rd20210006/),

[Royal Decree 93/2020 Amending the Name of the Ministry of Housing to the Ministry of Housing and Urban Planning, Determining Its Mandates, and Adopting Its Organisational Structure](https://decree.om/2020/rd20200093/),

[Royal Decree 42/2021 on Granting of Government Lands](https://decree.om/2021/rd20210042/),

[the Statute of Governorates issued by Royal Decree 36/2022](https://decree.om/2022/rd20220036/),

and [the Law on the Expropriation for Public Benefit issued by Royal Decree 71/2023](https://decree.om/2023/rd20230071/),

and after presentation to Majlis Oman,

and in pursuance of public interest,

### have decreed as follows

### Article I

The provisions of the attached Urban Planning Law hereby apply.

### Article II

The Minister of Housing and Urban Planning shall issue the executive regulation of the attached law, and shall also issue the necessary decisions for implementing its provisions. Until they are issued, the existing regulations and decisions continue to operate to the degree that they do not contradict with its provisions.

### Article III

All that is contrary to the attached law, or in conflict with its provisions, is hereby repealed.

### Article IV

This decree must be published in the Official Gazette, and comes into force on the day following the date of its publication.

**Issued on: 4 Dhu Al\-Hijjah 1447Corresponding to: 21 May 2026**

**Haitham bin TarikSultan of Oman**
