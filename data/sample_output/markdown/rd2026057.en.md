Arabic

We, Haitham bin Tarik, the Sultan of Oman

after perusal of the Basic Statute of the State ,

the Law Regulating Postal Services promulgated by Royal Decree 71/2012 ,

the System of the Administrative Apparatus of the State promulgated by Royal Decree 75/2020 ,

and Royal Decree 90/2020 Establishing the Ministry of Transport, Communications, and Information Technology, Determining Its Competences, and Adopting Its Organisational Structure ,

and in pursuance of public interest,

### have decreed as follows

### Article I

All allocations, assets, rights, obligations, and holdings relating to the postal sector are hereby transferred to the Ministry of Transport, Communications, and Information Technology from the Telecommunications Regulatory Authority.

### Article II

The officers of the Telecommunications Regulatory Authority competent with the postal sector are hereby transferred to the Ministry of Transport, Communications, and Information Technology with their same financial grades in accordance with the mechanism agreed upon between the Minister of Transport, Communications, and Information Technology and the Executive President of the Telecommunications Regulatory Authority.

### Article III

The mandate to regulate and issue licences for express and last-mile delivery via digital media (delivery applications) is hereby assigned to the Ministry of Transport, Communications, and Information Technology.

### Article IV

The word “authority” is hereby replaced with the word “ministry”, the phrase “chairman of the board” is hereby replaced with the word “minister”, the phrase “Telecommunications Regulatory Authority” is hereby replaced with the phrase “Ministry of Transport, Communications, and Information Technology”, and the phrases “chairman of the board of directors of the Telecommunications Regulatory Authority” and “executive president of the Telecommunications Regulatory Authority” are hereby replaced with the phrase “Minister of Transport, Communications, and Information Technology”, wherever they occur in the mentioned Law Regulating Postal Services and in laws and royal decrees relating to the postal sector.

### Article V

This decree must be published in the Official Gazette, and comes into force on the date of its issuance.

**Issued on: 1 Dhu Al-Hijja 1447 Corresponding to: 18 May 2026**

**Haitham bin Tarik Sultan of Oman**

Published in Official Gazette 1649 issued on 26 May 2025 .
